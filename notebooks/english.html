Task 1 · True / False (исправь ложные)

  1. Michaela’s philosophy is that high academic standards stop students from succeeding.
  2. Teachers at Michaela receive no training because they are expected to be naturally excellent.
  3. Detentions are given inconsistently, depending on each teacher’s mood.
  4. The school prioritises punctuality but does not punish lateness.
  5. Silent corridors are used in order to reduce fights and distractions.
  6. The school is located in an affluent area where students face few challenges.
  7. Students are encouraged to avoid eye contact when speaking to adults.
  8. The strict rules are intended to be mean rather than helpful.
  9. Soft skills such as eye contact are ignored in favour of academic skills alone.
  10. The school’s approach is designed to prepare students for future success.
Ответ · Task 1

1F 2F 3F 4F 5T 6F 7F 8F 9F 10T

  • 1 — high standards help success
  • 2 — teachers are trained / get feedback
  • 3 — detentions are consistent
  • 4 — lateness → detention
  • 6 — Brent, not affluent
  • 7 — eye contact is encouraged
  • 8 — rules are helpful / purposeful
  • 9 — soft skills are taught too
